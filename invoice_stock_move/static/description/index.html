Stock Picking From Invoice

This module allows users to create stock picking orders directly from customer/supplier invoices.

  • Overview
  • Features
  • Screenshots
  • Released Notes

This module empowers you to create stock picking orders directly from customer and supplier invoices. No longer are you confined to relying solely on sales or purchase orders to initiate stock movements. This streamlines your workflow, saving valuable time and resources.

Key Features

  • Stock Picking From Invoice
  • Stock Picking From Supplier bill
Stock Picking From Invoice & Bill

Allowing you to directly generate stock picking orders from customer and supplier invoices.

Improved Convenience

This functionality provides a more convenient way to manage inventory, especially for scenarios where invoices already contain all the necessary details for stock picking.
